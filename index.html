      On sourcing. Every entry here is an original wording written to carry an idea, not a quotation. None of these thinkers said these words. Each entry names the concept and the work it derives from so you can go and read the real thing. Where an idea is popularly attributed but not firmly sourced, the attribution line says so.

      On linking. Every view has an address. Filter, search or resize and the URL updates — copy it to send someone that exact slice. Single entries link as #e=bryant-06. Saving a quote works the same way — your saved list is written into the address, so bookmark the page to keep it. Nothing is stored on your device or sent anywhere.
